How It Works

Two stages. No mercy.

Most evaluation frameworks score everything. This one kills first. Only ideas that survive the constraint filter get scored — because scoring a bad idea wastes your time.

Stage 1
Kill Stage

7 non-negotiable constraints. Fail one, the idea is eliminated — no scoring, no partial credit. Designed to be brutal and fast.

Can this reach $2-5M ARR?
Operable solo, 10-15 hrs/week?
Revenue within 90 days?
No dependency on VC funding?
Can you own distribution?
Clear willingness to pay?
Defensible beyond 18 months?
Stage 2
Score Stage

8 dimensions, 5 points each. 32/40 is the GO threshold — calibrated against the 28-idea dataset, not invented.

TAM Clarity
CAC Efficiency
Solo Operability
Moat Strength
Time to Revenue
Domain Proximity
ARR Ceiling
Founder-Market Fit

Every evaluation produces one of three verdicts:

✓ GO — Score ≥ 32/40
◎ GO WITH CONDITIONS — 28–31, fixable gap
✗ NO-GO — Below 28 or constraint fail
