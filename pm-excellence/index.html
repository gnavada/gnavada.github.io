What's inside

Two files. One operating system.

pm-excellence is split into a universal skill and a personal calibration layer. The skill is world-class PM thinking. The personal layer makes it yours.

🧠

pm-excellence skill

The universal PM operating system: frameworks for decision-making, metrics architecture, stakeholder navigation, roadmap strategy, and research synthesis. Works for any PM at any company.

🎯

pm-excellence-personal

Your personal calibration layer: who you are, how you think, your current focus, your career trajectory, and your personal definition of a great week. Makes every Claude output specific to you.
