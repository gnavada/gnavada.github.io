Core principles

7 rules. Every message.

1

Lead with consequence

Never open with background. Open with what matters.

2

One ask

Every message has exactly one primary ask — make it impossible to miss.

3

Altitude match

Depth of detail calibrated to the decision the audience needs to make.

4

Earn every word

Remove any word that doesn't earn its place. Especially adjectives.

5

Name the tension

Don't pretend the trade-off doesn't exist. Name it first.

6

Separate feeling from fact

When you feel defensive, describe the data. When you feel uncertain, say so.

7

Close with clarity

End every communication with a clear next step or decision point.
